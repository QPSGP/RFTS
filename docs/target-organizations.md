# Reach For The Stars - Target Organizations & Groups

Prime candidate organizations and groups for RFTS members, organized by segment and
mapped to the three core personas in `docs/personas.md`:

- **Alex** - the Burned-Out Professional
- **Jordan** - the Front-Line Caregiver
- **Sam** - the Sleep-Deprived Parent / Caregiver

Each group notes **why they fit** and the best **entry path**: direct members,
affiliate referral, or facilitator/managed enrollment. See `docs/marketing-plan.md`
for the affiliate/facilitator model these paths rely on.

---

## 1. First responders & public safety (Persona: Jordan)

High stress load, shift work, hypervigilance, skeptical of "fluff" - a strong match
for the nightly nervous-system downshift.

- Police departments, sheriff's offices, and their peer-support / wellness units
- Fire departments and firefighter associations (e.g., IAFF locals)
- EMS / paramedic agencies and ambulance services
- 911 dispatch / emergency communications centers (often overlooked, very high burnout)
- Police & fire unions and benevolent associations (FOP lodges, PBA)
- First-responder chaplaincy and CISM (critical incident stress management) teams

**Entry path:** department wellness program partnerships → facilitator/managed
enrollment; union benefit add-ons; affiliate links via peer-support coordinators.

## 2. Healthcare & front-line medical (Persona: Jordan)

- Hospital systems' employee wellness / nurse resilience programs
- Nursing associations and unions (state nurses associations, ANA chapters)
- Traveling nurse agencies (irregular schedules, sleep disruption)
- Long-term care / hospice / home-health staff
- Medical residency & GME programs (residents are chronically sleep-deprived)
- Dental, veterinary, and pharmacy staff (high-burnout, under-served)

**Entry path:** hospital HR/benefits partnerships; affiliate outreach to nurse
educators and wellness officers.

## 3. Mental & behavioral health providers (affiliate + member)

Both prospective members and the best affiliate/facilitator recruits.

- Private-practice therapists, LCSWs, LPCs, psychologists
- Hypnotherapists and clinical hypnosis associations (natural lineage fit)
- Group therapy practices and community mental health centers
- EAP (Employee Assistance Program) vendors and provider networks
- Trauma-informed care clinics and PTSD programs

**Entry path:** affiliate program (recurring commissions) - they refer clients; some
become facilitators managing member content.

## 4. Corporate & high-stress professions (Persona: Alex)

- Tech companies (engineering, on-call/SRE teams)
- Finance, consulting, and law firms (long hours, high performers)
- Startups / entrepreneur communities and accelerators
- HR & benefits teams seeking low-effort wellness perks
- Sales organizations (high pressure, quota stress)
- Remote/hybrid workforces (isolation, blurred work-life boundaries)

**Entry path:** corporate wellness benefit; exec/leadership coaches as affiliates;
LinkedIn outreach to People/HR leaders.

## 5. Veterans & military (Persona: Jordan)

- VA medical centers and Vet Centers (wellness/complementary programs)
- Veteran service organizations (VFW, American Legion, DAV)
- Military family support & spouse groups
- Nonprofits for PTSD, moral injury, and reintegration

**Entry path:** nonprofit partnerships and facilitator-managed cohorts;
trauma-informed messaging.

## 6. Parents & caregivers (Persona: Sam)

- New-parent / postpartum support groups and lactation networks
- Mom communities and parenting Facebook groups
- Caregiver support organizations (dementia/Alzheimer's caregivers, e.g., Alzheimer's
  Association chapters)
- Special-needs parent networks
- Pediatric and family-practice clinics (referral affiliates)

**Entry path:** affiliate links via parenting influencers, doulas, family therapists;
Instagram/TikTok caregiver accounts.

## 7. Education (secondary segment)

- K-12 teachers & staff wellness programs and teachers' unions
- University counseling centers and student wellness offices
- Graduate students and student-athletes (performance + sleep)
- School nurses and administrators

**Entry path:** campus wellness partnerships; educator association benefits.

## 8. Recovery, support & 12-step communities

- Addiction recovery centers and sober-living programs
- Grief support and bereavement groups
- Chronic-illness and pain support communities (fibromyalgia, cancer survivorship)
- Sleep-disorder support groups (insomnia)

**Entry path:** facilitator-managed enrollment; affiliate referrals from counselors.

## 9. Faith & community organizations

- Churches, synagogues, mosques with wellness/care ministries
- Chaplaincy programs (hospital, corporate, public safety)
- Community centers, YMCAs, and senior centers
- Local nonprofits and mutual-aid networks

**Entry path:** community-leader affiliates (per marketing plan's "community leaders,
nonprofits" target).

## 10. Seniors & aging (secondary segment)

- Retirement communities and assisted-living activity programs
- AARP-affiliated groups and senior wellness initiatives
- Adult children caring for aging parents (overlaps with caregivers)

**Entry path:** activity/wellness directors as facilitators; sleep-focused messaging.

## 11. Coaches, studios & wellness practitioners (primarily affiliate/facilitator)

- Life, health, executive, and performance coaches
- Yoga & meditation studios, breathwork instructors
- Massage therapists, acupuncturists, and holistic wellness centers
- Personal trainers and gyms (recovery/sleep angle)

**Entry path:** flagship affiliate/facilitator recruits - they enroll and manage
member cohorts.

Work these first.

- [ ] **Veteran service orgs & PTSD nonprofits** - facilitator cohorts (Jordan)
- [ ] **Caregiver support organizations** (Alzheimer's/dementia, postpartum) - affiliate + community leaders (Sam)
