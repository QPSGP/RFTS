# Reach For The Stars — Top 3 Marketing Personas

Use these personas to steer blog topics, ad creative, short-form hooks, affiliate outreach, and landing-page copy. Each maps to segments in `docs/marketing-plan.md`.

---

## Persona 1: The Burned-Out Professional

**Name:** Alex (composite)  
**Age:** 32–44  
**Role:** Manager, knowledge worker, or entrepreneur — high responsibility, constant connectivity

### Snapshot
Alex is competent and exhausted. Evenings are for recovery, but the brain will not power down. They have tried apps, podcasts, and occasional meditation classes, but nothing sticks because it adds another task to an already full day.

### Goals on RFTS
- Stress relief, balanced life, better sleep
- May add health or wealth goals depending on season

### Pain points
- Racing thoughts at bedtime; wakes tired
- Guilt about not "doing wellness right"
- No time for 20-minute daytime meditation

### What they search for
- "guided meditation for stress"
- "can't fall asleep racing thoughts"
- "burnout recovery meditation"
- "meditation while sleeping"

### Messaging that resonates
- **No extra daytime chore** — press Start at bedtime; audios run while you fall asleep and during sleep
- **Evidence-informed, not woo** — hypnotherapy lineage, Terry's 50+ years, testimonials
- **Habit without willpower** — fixed nightly flow, personalized rotation

### Channels
- LinkedIn, Instagram (professional wellness), Google search (stress/sleep keywords)
- Therapist and executive coach affiliate referrals

### CTA
- 14-day trial → `/signup/step-1-subscription-selection`
- Landing: `/stress-relief`, `/sleep-meditation`, blog on burnout

## Persona 2: The Front-Line Caregiver

**Name:** Jordan (composite)  
**Age:** 28–55  
**Role:** Nurse, EMT, firefighter, police officer, or hospital/clinic staff — shift work or emotional load

### Snapshot
Jordan sees hard things daily and carries stress home. Sleep is irregular; hypervigilance makes it hard to feel safe enough to rest. They want practical tools, not vague "self-care" lectures.

### Goals on RFTS
- Stress relief, resilience, emotional health, sleep
- May use pain relief or balanced life depending on physical toll

### Pain points
- Shift schedules break routines
- Skeptical of gimmicks; values credibility and trauma-informed language
- Limited energy to learn complex programs

### What they search for
- "first responder stress relief"
- "sleep after night shift"
- "guided meditation for healthcare workers"
- "resilience meditation"

### Messaging that resonates
- **Built for real stress** — not spa-day fluff; nightly nervous-system downshift
- **Trusted voice** — Success Center lineage, facilitator network, 800-GOAL-NOW support
- **Works when you're depleted** — one button at bedtime; optional second audio during sleep

### Channels
- Facebook groups (first responder / nurse communities), union or department wellness programs
- Facilitator and affiliate partners in healthcare and public safety
- Short-form video: "60 seconds to downshift after a hard shift"

### CTA
- Trial via facilitator referral link (`?ref=CODE`) when assigned by a coach
- Landing: `/stress-relief`, `/resilience-meditation`, `/sleep-meditation`

## Persona 3: The Sleep-Deprived Parent or Caregiver

**Name:** Sam (composite)  
**Age:** 30–50  
**Role:** Parent of young children, or adult child caring for aging parents — fragmented sleep, mental load

### Snapshot
Sam's sleep is interrupted by others' needs, not choice. The only quiet moment is late at night, and that's when anxiety spikes. They want rest and emotional regulation without another subscription that requires daily active use.

### Goals on RFTS
- Sleep, stress relief, emotional health, balanced life
- May add relationship or health goals

### Pain points
- Interrupted sleep; hard to fall back asleep
- Scrolls phone in bed because it's the only "me time"
- Feels selfish taking time for wellness

### What they search for
- "guided sleep meditation for parents"
- "fall back asleep meditation"
- "caregiver stress relief"
- "sleep hypnosis when you can't switch off"

### Messaging that resonates
- **Self-care that doesn't steal from the family** — happens while you are already in bed
- **Replace the scroll** — same phone, better ritual: Start Session instead of feeds
- **Gentle and consistent** — intro relaxation music, then your goals; no perfection required

### Channels
- Instagram Reels / TikTok (parenting and caregiver accounts)
- Mom blogs, caregiver support groups, pediatrician or family therapist affiliates
- Blog + `/sleep-meditation` SEO cluster

### CTA
- Start your journey tonight — signup with trial
- Landing: `/sleep-meditation`, `/emotional-health`
